============================

Source: the game's own files --
  common/terrain/00_terrain.txt    terrain penalties and widths
  common/defines/00_defines.lua    stacking, rivers, forts, supply

Not wiki estimates; these match the build exactly.

STACKING (COMBAT_STACKING_*)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Divisions without penalty = 5 + 3 x (attack directions - 1)
  Each division over that   = -2% to the WHOLE attack
  So sending 10 divisions from one direction makes the attack 10% WEAKER.
  Open another direction instead. Each one raises both the limit and the
  combat width.

OTHER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  River crossing        small -30%   large -60%
  Forts                 -15% per level
  Entrenchment          +2% per level (to the DEFENDER)
  Encirclement          -30%
  Carried supply        72 hours (entering a pocket before this runs out is wasted)
